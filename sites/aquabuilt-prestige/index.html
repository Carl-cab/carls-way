Second Life

Renovations for vinyl liner and concrete pools.

An older pool doesn't need to be torn out to be brought back. Most vinyl and concrete pools can be renewed structurally and cosmetically, often for a fraction of a full rebuild.

Vinyl Liner

Liner Replacement & Refresh

New liners, updated patterns, and wall/floor prep to correct wrinkling, fading, or fit issues. We also address the coping, skimmer, and fittings while the pool is drained, so the same problems don't resurface in year two.

Concrete

Resurfacing & Structural Repair

Crack repair, replastering, and re-tiling for aging concrete shells, plus coping and deck restoration. Where the structure allows, we can also update the shape, add a tanning ledge, or extend the pool during the same renovation.
